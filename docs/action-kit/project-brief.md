# NGTA: request for focused research advice

Project: https://github.com/ArjunCodess/NGTA/tree/version-2

NGTA applies explicit prototype rules to a heuristic confidence signal in a clinical transformer's inference computation. It exports the conditions, truth-value revisions and attention/probability effects for independent numerical replay. It is a research prototype; clinical safety, predictive improvement and reviewer benefit are unestablished.

Development evidence includes 20 WiDS hospital-held-out fits and 15 TCGA clinical/genomic/fused fits, each condition evaluated across five training seeds. Matched inference and independent replay address earlier implementation confounds. The intended symbolic Brier benefit is not established, and missingness robustness criteria fail in many conditions. Results are reported as negative or inconclusive rather than tuned into a positive claim.

## The first request

A critical-care or thyroid specialist could review four predicates in their own area and return a dated retain/revise/reject decision with rationale. The review would cover clinical interpretation, units, timing, source support and correlated/shared evidence. It should separately assess the proposed numerical weights; clinical plausibility does not establish calibration.

The thyroid predicates concern recorded BRAF mutation, age at diagnosis of at least 55, pathological T3/T4 categories and recognized extrathyroid extension. The ICU predicates concern day-one maximum lactate of at least 4 mmol/L, minimum systolic blood pressure of at most 90 mmHg, age of at least 75, and maximum creatinine of at least 2 mg/dL. These are prototype probes, not treatment instructions or validated mortality/metastasis probabilities. Detailed source notes and review fields are in [the review form](../data-requests/rule-review-form.csv).

## Advice or referrals needed later

- A statistical collaborator could assess the locked comparisons, cohort uncertainty and proposed 24-reviewer/80-case demonstration design before a formal participant study.
- An institutional collaborator could advise whether and how an independent researcher can obtain the applicable ethics/research determination, consent process and authorized independent evaluation data. No approvals or data-access authority are claimed.

No patient records, participant responses or answer keys are attached. The initial request is a focused discussion or introduction, not a request to endorse the work, sponsor data access immediately or promise a favorable result.
